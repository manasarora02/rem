# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People who carry too many small commitments mentally — things to do, people to follow up with, things they're waiting on, promises made, deadlines approaching. Visiting the landing page to decide whether an early-stage AI personal manager is worth joining a waitlist for. They arrive skeptical of AI hype and need to understand the actual idea, concretely, in under a minute.

## Product Purpose

Rem is an AI personal manager, still in early development. You tell it what's happening in your life — commitments, follow-ups, things you're waiting on — and it remembers what matters and brings it back when it needs your attention, without you having to create a task or set a reminder yourself. The landing page's job is to make that idea land in 30–60 seconds of scrolling and convert to "Join Early Access" (a waitlist, not a full account) or "Follow the build."

## Brand Personality

Minimal, personal, calm, premium, intelligent, slightly futuristic, trustworthy. Explicitly transparent about being early-stage — this should read as an ambitious product being built carefully in public, not a mature company overstating what exists today.

## Anti-references

Excessive gradients, glowing AI effects, robots, floating particles, huge/identical feature-card grids, excessive glassmorphism, stock imagery, fake testimonials, fake customer logos, fake metrics, fake screenshots, pricing tables. Generic AI-marketing language ("revolutionize your productivity," "10x your workflow," "AI-powered productivity ecosystem").

## Design Principles

- Show, don't explain — the conversation demo should carry more of the product explanation than any paragraph of copy.
- Concrete over hype — write like a person explaining an interesting product to another person.
- Transparent about being early — "Rem is being built to..." / "The goal is for Rem to...", never claim finished functionality that doesn't exist.
- One idea per section, four sections only — no scope creep beyond what's needed to understand the product in 30–60 seconds.
- Restraint over spectacle — calm, confident minimalism instead of visually asserting "look how advanced this AI is."

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, all interactive elements keyboard-navigable, motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
